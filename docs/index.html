About these demonstrations

AIRPiano aligns hand motion with a known symbolic score. It is trained on piano performance recordings and applied to tabletop gestures without tabletop training data.

P1 and P2 appear in both piano and tabletop examples. P3 and P4 appear in tabletop examples. During test-set collection, tabletop participants followed a reference piano playback; the videos here replace that reference audio with the model’s output.
